# Take Home Assignment

As the next step in our founding engineer (Remote, Salary + Equity) interview process, we’d like you to redesign and implement the ChatBlu product landing page.

The goal is to evaluate your frontend execution, product thinking, design judgment, creativity, and ability to use modern development tools effectively.

Assignment:

  Redesign ChatBlu’s landing page (chatblu.ai) . The page should clearly communicate:

  - What ChatBlu does
  - Who it is for
  - The value it provides to hotels and their guests
  - Why it is different
  - What the visitor should do next

You’re welcome to change the page structure, messaging, interactions, and visual direction. You may use any frameworks, libraries, design resources, or AI coding tools.

 Please submit:

  - A working implementation or deployed link
  - Source code
  - A short README explaining your key product and design decisions
  - What you intentionally omitted or would improve with more time
  - The tools you used

Please time-box the assignment to approximately 3-4 hours. We care more about your decisions, execution, and ability to prioritize than the amount of functionality produced.

After this is done we'll reach out to schedule the next interview with our CTO and me.
